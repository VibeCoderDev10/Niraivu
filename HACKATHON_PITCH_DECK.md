# HACKATHON PRESENTATION & PITCH DECK
## Project: NIRAIVU (நிறைவு) — Drug Abuse Awareness, Prevention & Recovery Platform
**Vision: Towards a Drug-Free Tamil Nadu (போதையில்லா தமிழ்நாடு)**

---

## 🎤 2-Minute Pitch Script for Judges

### [0:00 - 0:30] The Hook & Problem Statement
"Good morning, judges! In Tamil Nadu, college students and youth face intense academic pressures and peer dynamics. Yet when it comes to substance abuse, the biggest barrier is **stigma, fear, and misinformation**. 
Traditional campaigns say *'Just say no'*, which doesn't work in a closed hostel room. And when a crisis or overdose happens, families panic because verified medical facilities and emergency lines are fragmented."

### [0:30 - 1:00] The Solution: NIRAIVU
"To solve this, we built **NIRAIVU** (நிறைவு) — meaning *wholeness and fulfillment*. NIRAIVU is a full-stack digital health platform engineered to bridge public health infrastructure with empathetic technology for youth."

### [1:00 - 1:40] 3 Core Features (Show the Live Screen)
1. **NIRA AI Guide**: 
   - Non-judgmental conversational assistant.
   - Built-in emergency triage: If a user types *"My friend is unconscious, looks like an overdose"*, it immediately displays an emergency red action banner with one-click **108 Emergency Ambulance** and **14446 Tele-MANAS** dialing.
2. **Interactive Tamil Nadu Care Locator**: 
   - Powered by Leaflet & OpenStreetMap.
   - Uses GPS geolocation with Haversine distance calculations to find verified centers across Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Salem, and more.
   - Features responsive mobile directory/map toggling so it never traps the user's scroll.
3. **Prevention & Scenario Simulator**: 
   - An interactive *"What would you do?"* challenge teaching youth the **CLEAR refusal framework** (*Calm, Look for exit, Emphatic no, Alternative, Remove*).

### [1:40 - 2:00] Closing & Impact
"NIRAIVU is built with React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Leaflet, running live on Vercel with automated GitHub CI/CD. We believe awareness can change a life, and recovery is 100% possible. Thank you!"

## 📊 Slide-By-Slide Presentation Content (Ready for Gamma.app / PowerPoint)

### Slide 1: Cover Slide
- **Title**: NIRAIVU (நிறைவு)
- **Subtitle**: Modern Drug Abuse Awareness, Prevention & Recovery Platform
- **Mission**: Towards a Drug-Free Tamil Nadu
- **Tech Stack**: React 19 • TypeScript • Tailwind CSS • Leaflet • AI Architecture

### Slide 2: The Problem
- High prevalence of peer pressure in college hostels and pre-exam stress.
- Deep societal stigma preventing students from asking questions or seeking clinical help.
- Inadequate refusal skills in traditional "Just say no" campaigns.
- Fragmented crisis information during overdose or withdrawal.

### Slide 3: The Solution
- **Awareness**: In-depth clinical substance guides & neurobiology of addiction.
- **Prevention**: Actionable playbooks for Students, Parents, and Friends.
- **Recovery**: Transparent 6-step roadmap (*Recognize -> Reach Out -> Get Support -> Medical Detox -> Counseling -> Rebuild*).

### Slide 4: Feature Spotlight — "NIRA" AI Assistant
- Confidential, compassionate 24/7 awareness guide.
- Emergency alert detection with direct **108 Ambulance** links.
- Strictly adheres to ethical safety boundaries (no advice on drug procurement/concealment).

### Slide 5: Feature Spotlight — Tamil Nadu Center Locator
- Real-time OpenStreetMap & Leaflet mapping.
- GPS distance calculations to verified government medical colleges and DMHP units.
- One-tap calling and Google Maps directions.

### Slide 6: Feature Spotlight — Scenario Decision Lab
- Practical decision simulations testing responses to party peer pressure.
- Teaches the **CLEAR Framework**:
  - **C**: Calm & confident
  - **L**: Look for an exit
  - **E**: Emphatic refusal
  - **A**: Alternative suggestion
  - **R**: Remove yourself safely

### Slide 7: Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite.
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 with dark navy & cyan/teal glassmorphism.
- **Mapping**: Leaflet + OpenStreetMap.
- **Deployment**: Vercel automated CI/CD directly synced to GitHub (`VibeCoderDev10/Niraivu`).

### Slide 8: Social Impact & Conclusion
- Destigmatizing addiction support for youth in Tamil Nadu.
- Accessible, verified, and life-saving in emergencies.
